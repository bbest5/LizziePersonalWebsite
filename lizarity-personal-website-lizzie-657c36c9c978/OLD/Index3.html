My plan

As a Learning Data Scientist in the IDEA Team at the Center for eLearning and Connected Environments (CeCE), I get to answer questions about online courses that impact college students and the University. College students have unique lives where their only option is to take online or hybrid courses. Researching the eLearning courses gives us information on how to make the courses a better experience. Doing data analysis will allow me to use queries to get information about eLearning courses that we need to make impactful decisions. Helping college students in a beneficial way is important to me because college gives us a space to better understand ourselves and the world, which is what I hope for people to be able to do.
